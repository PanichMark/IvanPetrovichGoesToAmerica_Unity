If the fellow is still abed, I shall throw him out on his ear, Lord forgive me!
Ivan! It is already past midday, and you are still lounging about! What sort of disgrace is this, upon my word?!
I shall lie abed for as long as I please.
You shall lie abed, my dear fellow, for precisely as long as you are blessed to do so!
And you were bidden to rise before eight o’clock, tidy up, and make everything ready for the service!
And who, pray tell, would ever wander into our memorial church—especially with renovations underway, by the by?
It is as empty here as a bare field. Leave me be, for Christ's sake!
"Leave me be"?! Why, you good-for-nothing! I will show you "leave me be"! Up with you this very instant!
Just take a good look at yourself: you are in your twenty-eighth year, yet you have neither a proper calling, nor a wife, nor a roof of your own—not a single thing to your name!
That is scarcely my concern. Let our executive authorities see to my sustenance, my government quarters, and a betrothed.
So that is how the wind blows, Ivan Petrovich! Have you gone and joined the Republican Duma, then—with the Narodniks?!
Up with you, lively now! You shall not spread sedition and idle chatter within the walls of a holy sanctuary!
