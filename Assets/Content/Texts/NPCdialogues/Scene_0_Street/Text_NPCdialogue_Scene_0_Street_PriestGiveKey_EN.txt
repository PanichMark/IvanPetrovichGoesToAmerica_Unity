Oh, God bless you, Ivan, you have truly helped an old man out!
I see that little rascal has quieted down and gone on his way—a great weight has been lifted from my soul.
I scarcely hoped we would be rid of him so soon, and my legs have gone quite numb, besides it is time to get back to the service.
Well, a bargain is a bargain—here is the key to the wicket gate, just as I promised.
Go with God, have a hearty meal, and do not dare be late for Vespers!
