Oh, Ivan, is that you? Look what brought you out here—no doubt you were heading straight to my bistro to fill your belly again!
I am in a bit of a fix here: I hauled all the rubbish out of the place, yet this wretched bin refuses to open—the lock is jammed tight!
Come along, my good fellow, put your back into it and help me conquer this lid, seeing as you have youthful strength to spare.
And afterwards, you can lend me a hand with one more little matter, just as a favour between friends.
Once we have sorted it out, we shall head right back to the bistro, and I will whip up a splendid house shawarma for you!
