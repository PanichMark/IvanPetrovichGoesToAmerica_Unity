One of the inner yards of the Resentimernaya District, like many others, it is densely packed with huts and household plots built by the urban peasants with by their own means.
During the process of denationalization of property, all the peasants assigned by the Bolsheviks to urban real estate were evicted, but remained living in the yards adjacent to the apartment buildings.
You need to get to the monorail station and leave this district.
