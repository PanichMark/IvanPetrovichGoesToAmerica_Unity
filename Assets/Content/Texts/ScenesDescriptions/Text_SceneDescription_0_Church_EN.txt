Alexander II Memorial Church — formerly the Church of the Righteous Job the Long-suffering near the Volkovskoye Cemetery.
It received its new name after the remains of Emperor Alexander II were transferred here from the Saints Pyotr and Paul Cathedral of the Pyotr and Paul Fortress, following its closure and demolition by the Bolsheviks in 1919.
You need to go outside and go eat at a café-bistro.
