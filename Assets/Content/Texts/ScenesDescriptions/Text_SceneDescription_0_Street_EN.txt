Resentimernaya Station — the terminus station of the monorail on the southern outskirts of Petrograd, located in the district of the same name.
It received its name in connection with the mass repressions of townspeople, nobility, and clergy carried out by the Bolshevik authorities in this part of the city during 1917–1921.
One of Petrograd's poorest districts, overpopulated by urban peasants.
